Deep Learning Revision (source: Mitesh Khapra)

Lecture-by-lecture revision notes for Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra's Deep Learning course: a brief history of deep learning, then the McCulloch–Pitts neuron, perceptrons and the perceptron learning algorithm. Each page summarises one lecture with interactive demos and links to its video. More lectures will be added.

All credit to Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra (Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Madras; co-founder of AI4Bharat). His Deep Learning course is one of the clearest introductions to the subject anywhere, and it is freely available: the lecture videos are on the IIT Madras B.S. Degree Programme playlist, and the IIT Madras course page is CS6910 Deep Learning. The structure, the story and the examples in these notes are his. These are my personal, unofficial notes; explanations, demos and the "going beyond the video" boxes are my own, so check the lectures for the definitive treatment.

Numbering: L1, L2, … follow the order of the videos in the playlist above. Maths and charts load from public CDNs.
