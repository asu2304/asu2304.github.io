Deep Learning Revision (source: Mitesh Khapra)

Lecture-by-lecture revision notes for Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra's Deep Learning course, all 67 lectures: a brief history of deep learning, perceptrons and sigmoid neurons, gradient descent, feedforward networks and backpropagation, optimisers from momentum to Adam, bias, variance and regularisation, pre-training and activation functions, and transformers. Each page summarises one lecture with interactive demos and links to its video.

All credit to Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra (Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Madras; co-founder of AI4Bharat). His Deep Learning course is one of the clearest introductions to the subject anywhere, and it is freely available: the lecture videos are on the IIT Madras B.S. Degree Programme playlist, and the IIT Madras course page is CS6910 Deep Learning. The structure, the story and the examples in these notes are his. These are my personal, unofficial notes; explanations, demos and the "going beyond the video" boxes are my own, so check the lectures for the definitive treatment.

Numbering: L1, L2, … follow the order of the videos in the playlist above. The videos' own titles use a different numbering (the first video is titled “L2”), so each page also notes its YouTube title number. The playlist's last video ("Analysis of K-Maps in Complex Boolean Logic Minimization") is from a different course, so it is not included. Maths and charts load from public CDNs.
