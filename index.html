Machine Learning Revision (source: Arun Rajkumar, DA5400)

Lecture-by-lecture revision notes for the DA5400 machine learning course: representation learning (PCA, kernels, kernel PCA), clustering and K-means, estimation (maximum likelihood, Bayesian, GMMs and EM), regression (least squares, projections, gradient descent, kernels, the probabilistic view, cross-validation, ridge and lasso), and classification (0-1 loss, k-nearest neighbours, decision trees, generative models, naive Bayes, the perceptron, logistic regression and support vector machines), then ensembles (bagging, boosting, plus a detour on gradient boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost), classification losses as one framework, and neural networks, with a final course map. Interactive demos on every page.

All 74 lectures (L1–L74) + 5 detour pages on gradient boosting · interactive demos · DA5400

Transformers and LLMs (source: Tengyu Ma, Stanford CS229)

Revision notes on large language models from four Stanford CS229 lectures by Tengyu Ma: tokenisation and the transformer, KV caches, grouped-query and sliding-window attention, mixture of experts, in-context learning and instruction tuning, then reinforcement learning from MDPs and policy gradient to PPO and RL with verifiable rewards for reasoning models. Interactive demos on every page.

4 lectures · 22 pages · interactive demos
