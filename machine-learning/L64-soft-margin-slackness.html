Lecture 64 · Support vector machines: reading the multipliers

Complementary Slackness for the Soft-Margin SVM

Solve the soft-margin dual, look at one $\alpha^*_i$, and you can tell where its point sits. $\alpha^*_i=0$: correctly classified, on or beyond its margin line. $0<\alpha^*_i<C$: exactly on the margin line. $\alpha^*_i=C$: on the line or on the wrong side of it, inside the band or misclassified, paying a bribe. Everything with $\alpha^*_i>0$ is a support vector.

Source: this lecture on YouTube (≈21 min). Previous: L63 · Next: L65.

1 · The conditions

Let $(w^*,\xi^*)$ solve the primal and $(\alpha^*,\beta^*)$ the dual. Each term added to the Lagrangian vanishes at the optimum:

$$\textbf{CS1:}\quad\alpha^*_i\,\big(1-(w^{*\top}x_i)y_i-\xi^*_i\big)=0\qquad\qquad\textbf{CS2:}\quad\beta^*_i\,\xi^*_i=0\qquad\qquad\text{for all } i .$$

Three more facts are always available: $\alpha^*_i+\beta^*_i=C$, $\ \xi^*_i\ge0$, and $(w^{*\top}x_i)y_i+\xi^*_i\ge1$. In the hard-margin case there were two cases ($\alpha^*_i$ zero or positive). Now $\alpha^*_i\in[0,C]$, and the top value $C$ is special, so there are three.

3 · Who are the support vectors now?

A support vector is any point with $\alpha^*_i>0$: it is needed to build $w^*=\sum_i\alpha^*_iy_ix_i$.

  • Hard margin: support vectors lie on the supporting hyperplanes, nowhere else.
  • Soft margin: they lie on a supporting hyperplane, or on the wrong side of it: in the "danger zone" between the hyperplane and the separator (correct but with margin below 1), or beyond the separator (misclassified, $(w^{*\top}x_i)y_i<0$).

The misclassified points are, in effect, the outliers: $w^*$ is being robust by leaving them on the wrong side, and they pay for it with $\xi^*_i>0$ and the maximum weight $\alpha^*_i=C$. The cap is what stops any single outlier from dominating $w^*$.

Key takeaways

  1. CS1: $\alpha^*_i(1-(w^{*\top}x_i)y_i-\xi^*_i)=0$. CS2: $\beta^*_i\xi^*_i=0$. Also $\alpha^*_i+\beta^*_i=C$.
  2. $\alpha^*_i=0\Rightarrow$ margin $\ge1$, no bribe.
  3. $0<\alpha^*_i<C\Rightarrow$ margin exactly 1, no bribe.
  4. $\alpha^*_i=C\Rightarrow$ margin $\le1$, bribe $\xi^*_i=1-(w^{*\top}x_i)y_i$.
